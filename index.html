Transforming Sketches & Tech Packs into Perfect Production Patterns

Hello! I am NASEEM AHMAD, founder of Tukadesignexpert. With over a decade of hands-on experience in garment construction and digital pattern engineering, I bridge the gap between creative fashion design and flawless factory production.

I specialize in using industry-leading CAD platforms—specifically **TUKAcad** and **Optitex** and **Gerber** 2D/3D**. Whether you need complex size grading, fabric consumption markers, or 3D digital fit simulations, I deliver high-precision pattern files ready for cutting rooms worldwide.

Specialized CAD Pattern Making Services

High-accuracy digital pattern solutions tailored for boutique labels, independent fashion designers, and large-scale garment factories.

Digital Pattern Drafting

Creating first-base digital patterns from tech packs, sketches, images, or physical sample garments using Optitex & TUKAcad.

  • Includes seam allowances & notches
  • DXF / Printable PDF format

Size Grading & Nesting

Professional size grading according to your custom measurement specs or standard size charts (US, EU, UK, Asia).

  • Grading Nest Overlay Charts
  • RUL & Cut File creation

3D Garment Fitting & Avatar

Virtual 3D garment simulation on custom avatars (TUKA3D / Optitex 3D) to check drape, fit, tension, and fabric fall before cutting.

  • 3D Fit tension map analysis
  • Saves costly physical sampling

Marker Making & Efficiency

Optimized marker nesting for automated cutters or manual cutting to maximize fabric yield and minimize waste percentage.

  • Fabric consumption calculation
  • PLT / HPGL plotter ready

Tech Pack to Pattern

Converting design specifications, measurement tables, and flat sketches into complete production-ready sewing patterns.

  • Measurement spec verification
  • Construction details integration

Pattern Digitizing & Conversion

Converting old hardcopy paper patterns or non-standard file formats into modern digital Optitex / TUKA formats.

  • Paper to Digital Conversion
  • A0/A4/Letter Home Printer PDF
